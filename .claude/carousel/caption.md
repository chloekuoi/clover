# Clover 1.0 — Instagram launch caption

Clover 1.0 is live 🍀

Ever sat in a café full of interesting people and talked to none of them?

Walking up to someone cold is hard. Tapping "invite to co-work" isn't.

Clover shows you who's actually working around you right now — check in, set your focus, see who's here, and send a co-work invite before your coffee's cold.

Made for anyone who works remote, builds solo, or just loves co-working.
