# Clover product vocabulary (for X posts that mention the app)

Use this only for the *nouns and product facts* when a post references Clover.
Chloe's personal, playful voice always sits on top — this keeps the product
consistent, it does not flatten her tone. Source: `.claude/brand-deck-rules.md`.

## What Clover is
- A **co-working discovery app**. Users set a daily work intent, browse nearby
  people working today, connect, and meet up. "Hinge for co-working."
- It is a **discovery app** — never call it a "social network."
- Tagline: **"Find your people. Do the work."**

## Naming rules
- In **formal / company contexts**: **Clover** — capital C, no article. "open
  Clover," not "open the Clover." Never ALL CAPS.
- In **Chloe's casual personal X posts**: she often writes **clover** lowercase,
  and that's fine — it reads natural and texty. Match her lead; don't force the
  capital C into her journal-style voice. (Capitalization matters for the
  company brand, not for how she casually types about her own app.)

## Words to use
- **builders** (the people on Clover) — never "users" or "customers"
- **co-work / coworking**
- **set your intent** (today's focus/task)
- **work side by side**
- **meet IRL**
- **connect** (not "network")
- **accountability** (not "productivity hack")
- **do the work**

## Words to avoid
- "users," "customers" → say **builders**
- "social network" → say **discovery app**
- buzzwords: synergy, leverage, ecosystem, disrupt, seamless, frictionless,
  best-in-class, "scaling" as a standalone flex

## Quick fact sheet (for accuracy when posting build updates)
- Stack: React Native + Expo, Supabase backend (in case she posts technical
  detail — don't invent specifics beyond what she tells you)
- Core loop: set intent → browse people working nearby today → connect →
  match → chat → propose a co-work session → meet up
- Features shipped so far include: discovery feed, matching, 1:1 chat, session
  proposals, friends, group chats, Apple Sign-In. Only reference a feature in a
  post if Chloe brings it up — don't announce things on her behalf.
