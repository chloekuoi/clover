The two cover types

"Now building" uses a soft lavender pill. "Hot take" uses deep violet and italic quotes, so it reads louder.

On your profile grid

The grid shows only the middle 3:4 of each cover. All text stays inside that band, so nothing gets cut off. The last tile is the no-photo version for teasers.

Colour logic

The fixed series brand sits in cream on Clover green. Whatever the guest says goes in lavender, because in your system lavender is the user's voice.

Shooting tip

Frame guests centred, from the knees or waist up, with their face in the top third. The wordmark then crosses their body, not their face.

Copy length

Keep descriptors to about 3–5 words ("promise tracker ring"). Longer hot takes wrap to 2 lines, which is the maximum.
